## P2 — the director

**What shipped.** `18-audio-director.js`: `createDirector({ attack, release,
weights })`, kit-audio (documented in `16-audio-engine.NOTES.md`; no version
bump, plan §8). `23-main.js`: top-level `dangerInputs(state, out)` and
`audioFrame()`'s director, duck and sweep lines. `19-sfx.js`: the `Director`
instance, `duckFor()`, and `sfx()`'s dip. `C`: `MUSIC_DIP_EVENTS`;
`INT_HEAT_MAX` deleted. Harness `EXPORTS` +4. `test-cs010-p2.js` (187).

**Readings taken, none a design call.**
1. **"A non-run screen" is read as: neither play nor pause side** (`duckFor`).
   Game over counts as non-run, so RESTART resets. A closure flag in `Game`
   (`audioRunLive`) holds the previous frame's answer; `Game.reset()` clears it,
   so a suite `reset(); startGame()` starts from 0 too. ⚠ A `startGame()` called
   while already on play (the pinned soaks' restarts) does NOT reset: the
   screen never left play. P5's soak should know.
2. **The director and all three setters run BEFORE `setState()`**, so a track
   that starts on a frame builds its gates at that frame's level (P3's tiers).
   `test-cs009-p3.js`'s pin (setState before update) is untouched.
3. **Game over sets nothing** (held), since D8 only names play, title and the
   pause side, and the music is fading out.
4. **The Dive's zero reading is a frozen all-zeros object**, not a skipped
   frame: the level falls by the release, and `dangerInputs` is not called.
5. **The weights object names all five inputs**, `combo` included at
   `C.INT_W_COMBO`, fed 0. The Classic ceiling is 0.85 (asserted), per D6.

**A consequence of R1, flagged, not changed.** The director's dt is the audio
clock's, and pause-side frames do not call it, so the first play frame after a
pause integrates the whole pause: after g seconds it moves the level
1 − e^(−g/τ) of the way to the reading (PREDICTED by arithmetic: a 5 s pause
covers 86 % of a release). The hold is exact while paused (asserted).

**✅ Paul's answer, same day: skip it.** Pause-side frames now call
`Director.hold()` (new: keep the level, forget the clock), so a resume's first
frame takes the clock and moves nothing. Asserted on the fake (the level after a
paused resume equals the level held) and in the unit case. **M11** — no `hold()`
on the pause side — is red (2); M3, M5 and M10 re-run and still red. 193
assertions.

**R4 (plan §11), observed on the fake, not heard.** Title → play: the reset
puts the level at 0, so the same frame targets the sweep at `FILTER_MIN_HZ` with
`FILTER_TC` 0.05 s while `title` starts its 0.6 s crossfade out. Its tail is
therefore darkened within ~0.15 s, faster than the plan's "over 0.6 s".

**Measured against predicted.** `test-cs007-p2.js` green with `heatT()` (E2, as
predicted). `test-cs009-p3.js` green. One self-inflicted red on the way:
`test-cs009-p5.js`'s seat scan read a `C` comment containing `sfx(`; reworded.
No other closed file moved. Build 486.9 KB (480.0). Suite 51 files, zero skips.
`test-cs006-p2.js` 1229033515, unmoved.

## P3 — the earned layers, Paul's gains, and music-lab

**What shipped.** `17-audio-tracks.js` (and both labs' BLOCK B): `bar` on both
tables; `cycle` tier 2 and `tick` tier 3 (D1); `pulse` at D3's gains, the trim

**Readings taken, none a design call.**
1. **sfx-lab needed `gating`, which the prompt did not name.** Its loader throws
   on a tiered table without it, so the lab would not have opened. It plays
   `pulse` at intensity 1 — every tier open, the mix the headroom gate
   measures — which is what "in context" meant before tiers existed.
2. **`bar: BAR`, not the literal `bar: 16`.** Same value (asserted 16); it cannot
   drift from the constant that shapes the rows.
3. **`tier` is written just before `audition`** (before `steps` on a line with
   no mark), so a later audition edit leaves the order alone. Asserted.
4. **A layer that leaves PASS loses its tier**, so the lab cannot print a tier on
   a non-PASS layer. INTENSITY starts at 1, so a solo audition hears every layer
   and the sweep open, as CS009's lab did.
5. **A lab TIER edit writes the live gate's `tier` and re-calls `setIntensity`**
   at the held level, so BLOCK A latches it to the bar line like any change.
6. **The p5 fixture repair keeps its route assertion alive.** Finding voices by
   route alone would make "the tone feeds the SFX bus" vacuous, so that
   assertion now walks every envelope carrying the recipe's oscillators,
   whatever its route. The headroom block's voice lookup got a fixture
   assertion: a re-routed tone crashed the file instead of failing (M14).
7. `test-cs010-p3.js` also pins music-lab's `LAB.limit`, `LAB.gating`,
   `LAB.sweep` and sfx-lab's `LAB.gating` to `C` (criterion 7 had no lab check).
