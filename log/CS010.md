**What shipped.** `18-audio-director.js`: `createDirector({ attack, release,

**Readings taken, none a design call.**
1. **"A non-run screen" is read as: neither play nor pause side** (`duckFor`).
   Game over counts as non-run, so RESTART resets. A closure flag in `Game`
   (`audioRunLive`) holds the previous frame's answer; `Game.reset()` clears it,
   so a suite `reset(); startGame()` starts from 0 too. ⚠ A `startGame()` called
   while already on play (the pinned soaks' restarts) does NOT reset: the
   screen never left play. P5's soak should know.
2. **The director and all three setters run BEFORE `setState()`**, so a track
   that starts on a frame builds its gates at that frame's level (P3's tiers).
   `test-cs009-p3.js`'s pin (setState before update) is untouched.
3. **Game over sets nothing** (held), since D8 only names play, title and the
   pause side, and the music is fading out.
4. **The Dive's zero reading is a frozen all-zeros object**, not a skipped
   frame: the level falls by the release, and `dangerInputs` is not called.
5. **The weights object names all five inputs**, `combo` included at
   `C.INT_W_COMBO`, fed 0. The Classic ceiling is 0.85 (asserted), per D6.

**A consequence of R1, flagged, not changed.** The director's dt is the audio
clock's, and pause-side frames do not call it, so the first play frame after a
pause integrates the whole pause: after g seconds it moves the level
1 − e^(−g/τ) of the way to the reading (PREDICTED by arithmetic: a 5 s pause
covers 86 % of a release). The hold is exact while paused (asserted).

**✅ Paul's answer, same day: skip it.** Pause-side frames now call
`Director.hold()` (new: keep the level, forget the clock), so a resume's first
frame takes the clock and moves nothing. Asserted on the fake (the level after a
paused resume equals the level held) and in the unit case. **M11** — no `hold()`
on the pause side — is red (2); M3, M5 and M10 re-run and still red. 193
assertions.

**R4 (plan §11), observed on the fake, not heard.** Title → play: the reset
puts the level at 0, so the same frame targets the sweep at `FILTER_MIN_HZ` with
`FILTER_TC` 0.05 s while `title` starts its 0.6 s crossfade out. Its tail is
therefore darkened within ~0.15 s, faster than the plan's "over 0.6 s".

## P4 — the rim pulse

**What shipped.** `heart` gains `beat: true` (hand edit in three files, before
the audition mark as P3's STATUS note asked, plus a two-line header comment in
all three). `19-sfx.js`: `noteBeat` into `BEAT_ONSETS`, a `Float64Array` of
`C.RIM_PULSE_RING` (8) filled with −∞, and `beatGlow(now)`; both return early
with no context, per the file's header. `audioFrame()` sets `rimGlow` on play
only; `Game.reset()` zeroes it. `drawWell(…, rimGlow)`.

**Readings taken.**
1. **`rimGlow` is read AFTER `MusicSys.update()`**, not at the top of
   `audioFrame()`. A late step clamps its onset to `currentTime`; read before
   the scheduler, that onset would light one frame late. Read after, a
   future onset is still dark.
2. **`C.RIM_PULSE_RING` is a fourth constant** the prompt did not name. The
   ring's size is a number, and `C` owns every number. 8 holds far more than
   the lookahead can: `heart`'s closest onsets are 0.25 s apart against a
   0.2 s window.
3. **Linear fall, from the latest REACHED onset.** A pending onset never
   replaces a reached one (asserted).
4. **The alpha cap means only the dim band's rim changes alpha.** Elsewhere
   `wellBaseAlpha` is 1 and the product caps at 1. That is the spec as written
   (D10: "capped"), not a defect; recorded in GDD §11.6 and as a knob in
   `SKIPPED-PLAYTESTS.md`.
5. **One harness change**: `_harness.js` EXPORTS gains `noteBeat`, `beatGlow`.

**The test.** Every one of 4,800 half-frames of a front-door `pulse` run is
compared against `expected(now)`, worked out from the onsets the real
`playNote` receives for `heart`. Frames that scheduled an onset ahead while the
reached reading was 0 are counted (trap 1). The glow is read off the rim's
drawn width. `draw()`'s state hash and draw count are taken at its
`clearRect`. Headless, a `drawWell` spy sees a glow of 0 on every frame.

**Measured on the way.** `Game.reset()` leaves the screen on play, so the
fixture's `frame(0)` schedules one real `heart` onset at t = 0 before
`quitToTitle()`. The play gate is what keeps the title dark. M3 showed 7 lit
title frames from exactly that onset.
