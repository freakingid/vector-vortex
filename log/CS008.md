## P1 — the rim fix: (A) + (B) + ε

Built from plan §2 on Paul's R1/R2. **What shipped.** (A), ordering A1:
`updateShots()` ages and filters, then fires, and the `SHOT_MAX` check reads the
post-retirement length. (B): the Vaulter, Carrier, Drifter and Surger climbs
clamp at `1 - C.RIM_CONTACT_DEPTH`, written as that expression (never
`this.killDepth`, which the Surger zeroes), and `Vaulter.atRim()` reads it too.
The `WeaverBolt` is untouched. (ε): `C.HIT_DEPTH_EPS` 1e-9, in `collideShots()`'s
band test only.

**Why four samples.** A shot moves ≈ 0.03205 per step, so a fired shot is tested
at 1.000, 0.968, 0.936 and 0.904 — all within `HIT_DEPTH_TOL` of 0.95, the first
only with ε (`|1 - 0.95|` is 0.050000000000000044). Four samples against a
four-step cadence is every phase; without ε or without (A) it is three of four,
the 18/24 the mutations produce.

## P6 — pause, Options and Credits (and Paul's follow-up)

**Built to plan §7 and U4–U6.** `"pause"` is RESUME / OPTIONS / QUIT TO TITLE
over the frozen board. OPTIONS is TELEMETRY, EXPORT, CONTROLS ›, CREDITS ›, BACK,
from the title and from pause. kit-input **0.5.0** (P5 had taken 0.4.0).

**How Escape pauses.** Rebinding Escape to `pause` fails — every pause source
would then back out of menus, and a tab hidden on START DEPTH would back out to
MODE. So Escape stays `back`, `back` in play pauses, and `p`, Start, the touch
target and the hidden page are a separate action that does nothing off play.

**Four defects the plan did not name, each fixed at its cause.**
1. A pause arrives inside `input.sample()`, after `syncScreen()`: a Fire held in
   play confirmed RESUME at once. Fix: `syncScreen()` again after the sample.
2. Purge backs out of pause and was a rising edge on the first play step. Fix:
   re-latch the Purge on resume.
3. "Don't drain while `pause`" still drained under OPTIONS from that pause. Fix:
   drain only on `play` and `gameover`.
4. Blur and `visibilitychange` fire in either order and blur's `reset()` cleared
   the hide. Fix: the hide is a flag carried across `reset()`.

**Measured.** No baseline moved. `test-cs008-p5.js`: two claims rewritten in

## P8 — the sixth soak, the sweep, and the close

### `scratchpad/test-cs008-p8.js` — the front door (100 assertions)

⛔ **A sixth file, not a widened one.** The five before it each start a run with
`startGame()` and drive `Game.update()`. This one is **one continuous session
from the boot title**: twenty cases, each title → PLAY → CLASSIC → START DEPTH
(1 / 5 / 9 by case) → play → game over → RESTART → play → game over → QUIT TO
TITLE. Every screen change is a keyboard press into the input sink, and the loop
is `Game.frame()` at half a step per frame, so the death freeze is real and each
frame is at most one step. `Date.now` is a counter, so every time seed is
replayable.

**The driver.** The soaks' recorded rotation with the wall-to-wall pin, fire held
from the first step and a Purge every 311. After 9,000 live steps it turns
passive (rotation only) — ⛔ the fire-holder has no death path on levels 1–4,
and this is how a run ends. It **releases every key the frame game over lands**
(`STATUS.md`, P5's hazard). The first draft pressed PLAY on the title's entry
step, which latches held keys, and every later press was one screen behind;
two live steps after boot fixed the fixture.

**The claims.** No exception, no NaN on any live step; per case, the depth
selected, RESTART keeping depth and mode on a new seed, and QUIT leaving no run.
Score never falls within a run and **equals the sum of its events on every play
step** — P2's item-8 oracle, extended with the Start Depth bonus (GDD §4.6's
literals). `lives <= C.LIVES_MAX` on every frame. The Start Depth bonus is found
as what a clear paid beyond §7's three, and asserted at most once per run and
only on the starting well. **The rim-arrival property**, observed at the shot
pass itself: `_harness.js`'s spy wrappers gained optional `before`/`after`
hooks (additive; P5's and P6's spies are unchanged). Before `collideShots()`,
every hunting Vaulter newly at the park depth in the Skimmer's lane, with an
uncontested shot in band (`HIT_DEPTH_TOL + 1e-9`, the test's own slack), is an
obligation. After the pass, it must be dead — killed before `collideSkimmer()`
can make contact.

**Measured (seed 20260914).** 347,028 live steps over 709,896 frames, ~24 s.
40 game overs, 20 restarts, segments 1,625–21,918 steps. 199 clears, 150 deaths,
376 Purge kills, 1,606 Thorn chips, **30 lives awarded, 26 Start Depth bonuses
paid**, max level 15, **peak lives 4**. Rim: 190 arrivals, 93 of them mid-hop;
**29 obligations, 26 with the shot on its fire tick at 1.000, all 29 killed.**
Zero of every failure count.

reverted):
