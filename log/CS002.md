# log/CS002.md — The loop, the Skimmer, shots, and all four input devices

Narrative build log for CS002. `STATUS.md` carries the current changeset's
one-line ledger; the reasoning for this closed one lives here.

---

## P1 — the loop and the input struct

Built the one mutable game object (the fields CS002 owns), the fixed-timestep
loop, and mouse + keyboard input. The loop clamps `dt` at `DT_CLAMP_MAX`, runs
at most `MAX_CATCHUP_STEPS` steps per frame, and ⛔ **discards** the surplus
past the cap rather than banking it — banked debt is the spiral of death.
`Game.update(dt)` and `Game.draw()` are separate and `update` never touches
the canvas, so the whole simulation runs headless. `Game.hitStop(s)` freezes
simulation time without stopping the frame loop and leaves no accumulator debt
behind it.

`04-input.js` is kit-shaped from commit one: it reads no `C` and no `state`,
takes every tunable through its factory's options, and splits a DOM adapter
(`attach()`) off a device-agnostic sink so the suite can replay a recorded
event list with no DOM. Mouse is one multiply — ⛔ no acceleration curve.
Keyboard is the §9.2 dual mode: a release inside `KEY_TAP_MS` totals exactly
one lane, a hold ramps `KEY_SPEED_MIN`→`KEY_SPEED_MAX` over `KEY_RAMP`. The
ramp is live from the first step of a press rather than gated behind the tap
window, which means the constants are coupled: the ramp integrated over
`KEY_TAP_MS` must stay at most one lane (≈0.76 at shipped values) or a tap
silently becomes "however far the ramp got." Recorded permanently in
`src/04-input.NOTES.md`, not just here, since it constrains any future retune.

CS001 P3's debug well-cycler listener was deleted; well-cycling became the
named action `cycleWell`, dispatched by the input module during `sample()` —
⛔ one input path.

Verified: identical state hash over 10,000 ticks of one recorded event list,
twice in-process and once across processes; a 2-second stall runs exactly
`MAX_CATCHUP_STEPS` steps; mouse response exactly linear at nine magnitudes
with exact doubling; tap = one lane, hold monotone to `KEY_SPEED_MAX`; and a
source scan of the built file finds no `addEventListener`, `e.key`,
`.touches`, `getGamepads`, `movementX` or `clientX` outside the
`04-input.js` slice, and no `C.` inside it.

## P2 — the Skimmer

`lane` is a continuous float the simulation never rounds: closed wells wrap,
open wells clamp, and every lane arithmetic goes through CS001's
`laneNormalize`/`laneDelta` — no two lane floats are subtracted by hand. Snap
assist engages after `SNAP_IDLE_MS` of quiet, pulls at `SNAP_STRENGTH` toward
the nearest lane centre, and stops inside `SNAP_EPSILON`. ⛔ It is dead while
`input.rotate !== 0`. Two traps handled where they live: the seam target comes
back through `laneNormalize` and the direction through `laneDelta` (short
way); the snap step is capped at the remaining distance, which simultaneously
stops overshoot oscillation and stops a pull past an open well's clamp.

The `WALL_SQUASH_MS` squash is a count-up timer that ages *before* movement
each step, so the impact step renders at full squash rather than losing 42%
of it to `dt`. ⛔ It never writes `lane`.

Two judgment calls: `SKIMMER_COLOR` (`#FFFFFF`) isn't specified anywhere in
the GDD — white was chosen for legibility (pillar P1) and is distinct from
every band colour except White at levels 97–99, past any realistic ceiling.
Recorded in GDD §4.1 with a ⚠. And the Flat well (11) is geometrically
degenerate — its rim is a straight line, so its centroid sits on the rim and
`wellThroat` collapses to zero depth. Nothing in CS002 caused it; it's visible
now because P2 is the first phase to draw something standing in a well. Not
fixed here — it's the same design question as GDD §3.3's undefined
`throatOffset` (an offset throat is exactly what would give a straight rim
depth), and both are carried forward as open design questions for Paul,
landing naturally with CS004 (well progression).

Verified, 99 assertions: seven wrap cases on the 16-lane Ring including both
directions over the seam and multi-lap deltas; a 5,000-tick soak of
adversarial input on each of the six open wells never puts `lane` outside
`[0, lanes-1]` or non-finite; snap correctness (settles inside `SNAP_EPSILON`,
never re-moves after, never crosses the seam backwards, never leaves an open
well's clamp); the squash peaks at 1 on impact and decays to 0 without
touching `lane`; no NaN in the craft's projected points on any well.

## P3 — firing and shots

A shot's lane is captured once, at fire time, from `Math.round(skimmer.lane)`
through `laneNormalize` — the same target `Skimmer.snap()` uses — and never
changes afterwards. `state.shotCooldown` counts up toward `SHOT_COOLDOWN`,
starting already at the threshold (ready), the same pattern `squashTime`
opens on. `updateShots` ages/spawns/retires shots with an end-of-frame
`.filter()`; cap enforcement is a plain length check, not a preallocated pool
— consistent with every other entity's class+filter lifecycle. Draw split
into `14-render-entities.js`: a short rim-ward streak through
`drawPoly`+`glowStroke`, fading linearly from opaque to nothing as its
leading edge crosses `READABILITY_DEPTH` toward the throat (§10.3). No
collision pass, no Thorn chipping — explicitly out of scope.

Verified, 37 assertions: held fire for 10,000 ticks never exceeds `SHOT_MAX`;
a 4,000-tick soak proves no two shots are created less than `SHOT_COOLDOWN`
apart; a shot fired from a fractional lane locks and never moves afterward,
even under 200 ticks of adversarial rotation; a shot at depth 0 retires on
its own next `update()`; no NaN in `drawShot`'s projected points.

## P4 — touch, gamepad, feel-lab · closing phase

Touch is a relative drag — not a virtual stick — in world-space coordinates;
`attach()` is the only place that converts a real `Touch`'s client pixels
into that space, so the sink stays exercisable with no DOM. The drag is gated
to the bottom `TOUCH_ZONE_FRAC` of the well; Purge (top-right) and Jump
(bottom-right) are circular buttons at radius `TOUCH_BUTTON_R`, mirrored to
the left edge under `INPUT_MIRROR`. `TOUCH_AUTOFIRE` holds `fire` for as long
as a rotation drag is active — no separate fire button, per GDD §9.3/14.2's
explicit coupling to the Jump button.

Gamepad: the left stick is a held analog position, not a discrete press, so
it gets its own accumulator — proportional past `GAMEPAD_DEADZONE`, scaled by
`GAMEPAD_SENS`. The Gamepad API has no motion events, so `pollGamepads(win)`
polls once per `sample()` rather than listening for one. ⛔ The D-pad
(buttons 14/15) is NOT a second axis model — it is wired as two synthetic
bound keys (`bindSynthetic("gamepadleft", "left")`, etc.) fed through
`keyDown`/`keyUp`, so it rides the exact same `advanceAxis`/`releaseAxis`
state machine the keyboard uses. The test proves this bit-for-bit (matching
per-tick trajectories, not just totals), which is the real content of "one
code path, not two that agree today."

GDD §9.4 names no button for fire/purge/jump, and the P4 phase prompt didn't
either — rather than invent a mapping silently, the first cut shipped with
gamepad able to rotate and D-pad-tap/hold but not fire. After the on-hardware
pass, Paul's call: `gamepadButtons` (default `fire:[0]` (A), `jump:[4,6]`
(LB/LT), `purge:[5,7]` (RB/RT)), host-overridable wholesale like `keys`, and
configurable so a future settings screen can rebind it without touching
`04-input.js`. Tracked in its own `gamepadHeld` object rather than the shared
mouse/touch `buttons` Set — a polled level-state source must not blindly
clear a name another device (say, a held mouse button) is still holding.

`tools/feel-lab.html` measures rather than demonstrates: a duplicated slice
of the response curves plus CS002 P2's snap assist (held fixed; it isn't what
this phase sweeps) drives a synthetic "competent player" policy toward a
target a third of the well away, and reports time-to-target, overshoot, and
settle time across a range of `MOUSE_SENS`, `KEY_TAP_MS`, `KEY_RAMP`, and
`GAMEPAD_SENS`. `KEY_TAP_MS` shows identical rows across its whole range by
construction — a multi-lane traversal is a hold from the first tick and never
enters the tap window, which only governs the single-lane precision move GDD
9.2 defines it for. `tools/serve-lan.js` (`npm run serve`) serves it and
`dist/` over the LAN, since `file://` on a phone isn't a test.

**Constants shipped unchanged** from `feel-lab`'s readout — the default
values already satisfied the traverse-and-stop criterion on real hardware, so
no retune was needed: `MOUSE_SENS` 0.022, `KEY_TAP_MS` 130, `KEY_SPEED_MIN/MAX`
4.0/14.0, `KEY_RAMP` 0.35, `TOUCH_SENS` 0.030, `TOUCH_ZONE_FRAC` 0.40,
`GAMEPAD_DEADZONE` 0.15, `GAMEPAD_SENS` 12.0.

**On-hardware pass — PASS on all four devices**, 2026-08-30 (Paul): mouse and
keyboard on the laptop, gamepad on the USB pad, touch on a phone over LAN via
`tools/serve-lan.js`. Rotation and landing on the intended lane held up on
real hardware for all four, matching GDD §9's requirement.

Verified, 96 assertions in `test-cs002-p4.js`: a synthesized touch drag
matches mouse motion scaled by `TOUCH_SENS`/`MOUSE_SENS`; stick input inside
`GAMEPAD_DEADZONE` is exactly zero; the D-pad's per-tick trajectory matches
the keyboard's bit-for-bit for both a tap and a hold; every new option is
required (no silent default); gamepad buttons fire/jump/purge per the default
map and are host-remappable; a gamepad disconnect clears only gamepad-held
buttons, never another device's.

CS002 closes here. 24 modules, 9 test files, zero skips. The Skimmer,
shots, and all four input devices are load-bearing on shipped code; there is
still no collision pass and no enemy — CS003 is where a Thorn or an enemy
first becomes hittable.

## Carried forward (not CS002's to fix)

- GDD §3.3's `throatOffset` is undefined, and the Flat well (11) is
  geometrically degenerate for the same underlying reason. Design call for
  Paul; natural landing spot is CS004 (well progression).
- Devices are drained during hit-stop (`input.sample()` runs, `update()`
  doesn't) — intentional so a freeze doesn't dump banked mouse motion on
  resume, but CS006 (owns the death sequence) should confirm the reading.
- `src/04-input.NOTES.md` backport status is `not yet` — all four devices now
  exist, so this is the natural point to backport `kit-input` to
  coinless-kit, as a separate manual step.
- `SKIMMER_COLOR` (`#FFFFFF`) is a placeholder recorded with a ⚠ in GDD §4.1,
  not a final art decision.
- `pollGamepads()` reads gamepad index 0 only — no local multiplayer or
  pad-selection UI exists to need more.
