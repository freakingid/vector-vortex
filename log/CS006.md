# log/CS006.md — The well ends: a beat between wells, and two wells that could not be seen

Narrative build log for CS006. `STATUS.md` carries the current changeset's
one-line ledger; the reasoning for this closed one lives here.

CS005 finished the roster. CS006 is the changeset about what happens at the two
ends of a well — the moment one is cleared, and the moment a level number stops
having a table row to look up. Four systems, none of them an entity, and the one
thing they share is `23-main.js`: past-99 progression and the colour-band roll
(P1), `throatOffset` and the two degenerate wells (P2), the Dive (P3), and
`laneState` with the dim band (P4).

⛔ **This changeset began by splitting itself.** The old CS006 carried nine
systems; the seam is between *the well* and *the run*, they share one file in
two different functions, and both halves move `test-cs004-p1.js`'s golden
sequence. P0 took the split and swept the tail. **The run half is the new
CS007.**

⛔ **`log/CS003.md` and `log/CS005.md`'s changeset numbers predate one or both
renumbers, and a closed log is never revised.** The same is now true of this
file. **Note, do not rewrite.**

Six phases.

## P1 — past-99 progression, `state.bandRoll`, and a new invariant

**The trap was confirmed before anything was built.** A counting proxy over
`state.rng` showed `Game.draw()` spends **zero** draws today, and `Game.frame()`
runs 0–5 updates against exactly one draw — during hit-stop, ~72 draws against
zero updates. ⛔ **Nothing in the draw path may call `state.rng()`** is now an
invariant in `CLAUDE.md` under Math and lifecycle, reasoned in
`RATIONALE.md#draw-path-rng`. The symptom of breaking it reads as a physics bug,
not a rendering one, which is why it is written down rather than remembered.

`nextWell()` gained the `state.level > C.BAND_RNG_LEVEL` branch: **exactly two
draws**, the shape index then `state.bandRoll`, and the `else` branch spends what
the old modulo line spent, which is nothing. ⛔ **Nothing was added to
`enterWell()`** — its third caller is the `w` debug key, and a draw there would
let a keypress shift the run's stream. `Game.draw()` passes `state.bandRoll`;
`wellBandColor` takes a **number**, never a stream.

⚠ `state.level` itself does **not** hold past 99 — the derived band table does.
The consequence for CS007 is recorded at the field in `02-state.js`: §17 item 7's
`heat(n+1) > heat(n)` stays literally true only if `heat()` never plateaus.

**One closed test was edited**, and Paul's call was asked and given:
`test-cs005-p3.js` pinned the literal `drawWell(ctx, well, state.level, null, 0)`
including a fifth argument that was never CS005 P3's to own. Narrowed to the
argument the claim is about. ⛔ **P4 breaks the same assertion again** (`null` →
`lit`) and is what legitimately retires it.

## P2 — `throatOffset` defined, and the two wells that could not be seen

All sixteen wells were swept for the shortest lane-**centre** spoke *before*
anything was touched, and the planned numbers held: Flat 23.6 px, Stair 30.4 px,
then a gap to Twist 73.9 and Double-Vee 76.5, everything else ≥ 85.

GDD §3.3 now carries the field's definition, and it is asserted rather than
commented: ⛔ **a translation of the throat polygon in normalized rim space,
applied AFTER the centroid scale**, DATA, never written at runtime because
`wellThroat()` memoizes. The two consequences that pin it are the throat
polygon's centroid being the rim centroid plus the offset, and every throat
vertex still sitting at exactly `throatScale` of its rim vertex's displacement —
a scale applied after a translate fails the second.

The Flat gets `{x: 0, y: -0.50}` (23.6 → **151.8 px**, ratio 1.98), the Stair
`{x: 0, y: -0.35}` (30.4 → **79.6 px**, ratio 4.84). Both are the computed
candidates, and both land inside the max/min family the other fourteen occupy
(widest shipped: Double-Vee 4.25).

⛔ **`C.MIN_LANE_SPOKE_PX` 60 lands as a GATE, not a tunable.** A well that fails
it is redrawn or offset; the constant is never lowered to admit one. It
separates the two wells this phase fixed from the tightest working well with no
well inside 20 % of the line.

⚠ **A SECOND CLOSED TEST WAS EDITED AND THE PHASE PROMPT SAID NONE MAY BE.**
`test-cs001-p2.js` derived the throat as `centroid + (rim − centroid) ×
throatScale` with **no offset term** — exact on all sixteen wells until this
phase gave two of them a nonzero offset, and unsatisfiable afterwards by *any*
implementation of the field the same prompt required. The two instructions
cannot both hold, and the only way to keep the file untouched was to land no
offsets, which is the phase. `+ offX` / `+ offY` and nothing else: same sixteen
wells, same 1e-12, same message, no narrowing. The stronger offset-aware form —
decomposed into *translates* and *does not rescale* — is in `test-cs006-p2.js`
for all sixteen wells, so no coverage rests on the edit. Full record in
`DECISIONS.md`, 2026-08-30.

## P3 — the Dive

`src/11-dive.js` is the whole feature, and it ⛔ **REPLACED** CS003 P2's
one-second between-wells hold rather than joining it: the constant, the `state`
field and the branch are deleted, and neither name survives anywhere in the
built file — asserted against `dist/`, comments included, because a placeholder
that outlives its replacement is exactly what the assertion exists to catch.

⛔ **The Dive short-circuits the gameplay pass.** While one runs there is no
spawner, no entity pass, no shot pass, no Purge, no collision pass and no clear
check — all proved by counting proxies rather than by reading the source. Its
branch sits **below** the game-over stop and **below** `const well`, because a
dive death that ends a run must stop the dive too, and because the Dive reads the
**outgoing** well.

⛔ **`startDive()` filters the board to `anchored`, not to Thorns.** The
tempting reasoning — "a well is cleared, so only Thorns are left" — is false:
`WeaverBolt` ships `blocksClear = false` deliberately, so a well clears with one
travelling, and it carries the rim-band `killDepth` toward the rim the player is
leaving. The case that proves the filter has a live bolt on the board at clear.
`anchored` is the honest read because it means "`depth` is a length", and the
Dive's hazard *is* the lane extent.

Two beats: grace `C.DIVE_GRACE` 0.35 s at depth 1 with ⛔ **no strike test**,
then a 2.25 s descent 1 → 0. ⛔ The grace beat is a pillar P2 requirement, not
polish — `THORN_MAX` is 1.00, so a full-length Thorn's tip sits at the rim and a
dive beginning in that lane would otherwise be a death on step one.

The strike is `dive.depth <= thorn.depth` — ⛔ **the build's only two-depth
comparison**, which is why it lives in `11-dive.js` and not in the one collision
pass, whose header's "there is no term here for where the Skimmer is" has to stay
literally true. It routes through `killSkimmer()`, so item 5 inherits the
invulnerability guard, the life, the purge re-latch, the freeze and the stop
without writing any of them.

⛔ **A dive respawn lands in the nearest Thorn-free lane** by a deterministic
outward walk, ties toward increasing lane; and ⛔ **if every lane is thorned the
struck Thorn dies** — the termination guarantee, and the only non-shot path to a
Thorn's death in the build. A fully thorned Fan terminates at the cost of exactly
one life and one Thorn. ⛔ A dive spends **zero** RNG draws, transition included.

⚠ **Seven closed test files were touched and the prompt scoped four.** The four
it named were edited as instructed. The other three plus one more —
`test-cs003-p3.js`, `test-cs003-p4.js`, `test-cs004-p3.js`, `test-cs004-p4.js` —
are ⛔ **fixtures, not assertions**: each built a quiet board by draining the
quota to zero, and a drained quota with no `blocksClear` survivor is now a
**cleared well**, so the Dive ate the board before the case could assert on it.
Every repair is the same one line, `spawn.remaining = 1` instead of `0`, which
restores the precondition the assertion was always about. ⛔ No assertion was
weakened or deleted. `test-cs003-p5.js` needed one more: its soak's first
game-over moved from inside 10,000 ticks to tick 10,091, because a dive is ~1,100
safe ticks per 10,000 — `TICKS` is 12,000 there now, which strengthens §17 item 1
rather than relaxing it.

⚠ **The permitted red landed on the other baseline and the prediction was
inverted.** See P5.

## P4 — `laneState` wired, and gated to the dim band

The alpha arithmetic was confirmed before anything was built: a lit spoke draws
at `max(baseAlpha, C.LANE_LIT_ALPHA)`, and outside 65–80 that is `max(1.0, 0.9)`
= 1.0 — ⛔ **byte-identical to unlit**. So `Game.draw()` calls the producer only
when `wellBaseAlpha(state.level) < 1`, reading **the renderer's own predicate**,
so producer and consumer cannot disagree about where the band is. Lane lighting
is visible in sixteen levels of ninety-nine; an unconditional per-frame
per-lane pass would spend §17's budget on a provable no-op for the other
eighty-three.

⛔ **The renderer is unchanged** (comments only). This phase is
`buildLaneState(state, well)` in `23-main.js` and a new `C.LANE_LIT_MAX_LANES`
16. Three flags per GDD §3.7, containment `|laneDelta| < 1` so a boundary rider
lights both lanes and a closed well wraps.

⛔ **Every slot is cleared, not just `well.lanes`** — `drawWell()` indexes
`laneState[lanes]` on an open well's last spoke, and preallocation turns that
from `undefined` into a stale entry, so a wider well's flag would light a
narrower well's end spoke.

⛔ **The Surger's telegraph did not move onto it.** `surgeCharge` is a `Surger`
instanceof test, not a `phase` duck-type; a Weaver posed with `phase =
"telegraph"` sets nothing. The two marks are different kinds — `isLaneLit()` is a
boolean and cannot express `drawSurgeLane()`'s progressive fill.

The closed test P1 scheduled was rewritten in place: `test-cs005-p3.js`'s source
pin is now on `lit,` plus a pin on the producer's body proving the telegraph did
not move onto it. ⛔ The claim is unchanged — it was never "laneState is
unwired", it was "the telegraph is not laneState".

## P5 — the soak, the re-record, the close

### ⛔ A fourth soak, not an edit to the other three

`scratchpad/test-cs006-p5.js`, 59 assertions. Same reason CS005's close gave for
writing a third: a soak asserts its own changeset's board, and none of the other
three was written to say anything about a beat that did not exist. It extends
`test-cs005-p5.js` — same mixer, same recorded input list, same NaN walker, same
`replayWide` pin, same two fixtures — and deliberately restates none of its lane
assertions, its lattice cases or its per-well §17 item 3 soak.

⛔ **The hash folds in `dive.active` and `dive.phase`, not only the two numbers
`test-cs005-p5.js` already carries.** `timer` and `depth` alone would pass over a
build whose short-circuit had stopped short-circuiting: the beat would still
count, the gameplay pass would run underneath it, and the hash of the beat would
be unchanged. The dive's phases are a **separate namespace** from the entity
phase table — folding both into one index would let a Weaver's `hold` and a beat
collide.

⛔ **The hashed run is asserted to have COMPLETED a dive**, expressed as "`active`
fell and the level advanced", which is the only exit `updateDive()` has. Measured
inside the 10,000-tick window at seed 20260830: **several dives, 632 dive ticks**,
the descent beat reached.

### ⛔ The enemy array across a dive, in both directions

`startDive()` **filters** `state.enemies`, and a filter that kept the wrong side
shows up as a board that **empties**, never as one that grows. So both halves are
asserted and both are mutation-checked:

- **never grows** during a dive — nothing is spawned under a short-circuited
  pass, so the array can only shrink, and only by the termination guarantee;
- **every entity alive during a dive is `anchored`**, read off the contract
  field;
- **at least one dive began with a survivor on the board** — 16 of 50 across the
  twenty runs. Without this, every hazard assertion passes on an empty board.

Plus: no shot exists at any point in any dive (GDD §5's ⚠ SETTLED clear, and
nothing fires under the short-circuit), and `dive.depth` never leaves `[0, 1]`.

### ⚠ The death-loop bound is a BOUND, and it is not mutation-caught

The `≤ 2` guard is the phase prompt's, it is measured at **exactly 2**, and it is
honest to say what it does and does not catch. Replacing `diveRespawn()`'s guard
with the naive version GDD §5 describes — respawn in the lane it died in, no
Thorn destroyed — leaves `test-cs006-p5.js` **green**. The reason is the
scripted player: `replayWide` slams the craft wall to wall, so it rotates out of
the thorned lane before the next strike, and the loop the guard exists to close
never forms on a rotating board.

⛔ **The mechanism proof is `test-cs006-p3.js`'s and it is mutation-sensitive** —
staged, deterministic cases: the nearest-free-lane walk with its tie-break, the
fully-thorned Fan terminating at the cost of one life and one Thorn, and 100 runs
of one seed landing in the same lane.

⚠ **A live-board landing-lane assertion was tried and rejected, measured.**
Checking that the craft's lane on the respawn tick is Thorn-free is unsound:
`diveRespawn()` runs *before* `skimmer.update()` in the same step, so the craft
has already rotated off the chosen lane — observed at lane **0.702** at seed
21308120, one of only three post-strike respawns in the whole soak. The
observation would be of a lane the guard never chose. Recorded so a future
session does not rediscover it as a flake.

### ⛔ A passive run terminates — and it terminates by dying, not by diving

The phase prompt's reasoning was that "the dive is the first thing in the build
that can advance a level without a kill", so a passive run might stall in one.
Measured, it is the opposite: a well clears on *quota spent **and** nothing that
blocks the clear alive*, and a player who never fires kills nothing, so **a
passive run never reaches a dive at all** — `state.level` is 1 at the stop on
every seed tried, and the stop arrives well inside the cap: 1,085 ticks at worst
across the four seeded passive runs, against a 30,000 cap. The Dive cannot rescue
a passive run; the game-over stop is what ends it. The assertion stands as
written and the claim behind it is corrected here.

### ⛔ The count-based form of the no-draw rule

`PLANNED-FEATURES-CS006.md`'s H6 asked for the golden's guard role to be
*replaced* rather than re-recorded, and this is it. A recorded lane sequence is
weakest at the moment somebody re-records it; a count needs no baseline and
survives every retune. Decomposed, so a stray draw is caught at the component
that grew:

| Counted directly on the shipped function | Draws |
|---|---|
| `pickSpawnKind` on the shipped one-entry list | **0** |
| `pickSpawnKind` on a two-entry list | 1 |
| `spawnEnemy` (the heading) | **1** |
| `pickSpawnLane`, uncrowded | 1 |
| `pickSpawnLane`, every lane crowded | **exactly `C.SPAWN_LANE_TRIES`** — bounded, and it settles |

And end to end, per tick, over a live run: **exactly 2 draws per interval spawn**
on the one-entry list, **3** on a two-entry list of the *same kind* (the control
that makes "no third draw" mean something — it spends the kind draw without
putting a splitting Carrier or a laying Weaver on the board), **0** on every tick
that spawned nothing, and **0** across every one of 632 dive ticks.

⚠ Only the `k = 1` case of `pickSpawnLane`'s redraw appears in live play:
`C.SPAWN_INTERVAL` is 1.60 s, so an enemy has climbed out of the throat zone
before the next beat. The upper bound is therefore proved on the function with a
staged fully-crowded board rather than waited for.
