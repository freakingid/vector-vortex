**Calls made while building, none of them design.**
- *Where the cap check sits.* The draw is spent first, then the drop test, then
  the `MAX_TOKENS` refusal — so the draw is unconditional by construction, and
  the mutant that moves the cap check above the draw is the one that reddens the
  count. T2 says the board at the cap "still spends it"; the order is how.
- *`updateTokens()`'s order inside a token:* rise, age, expire, collect. A token
  whose age reaches `TOKEN_LIFE` on a step is gone that step and cannot be taken
  on it, which is what makes "every live token is younger than `TOKEN_LIFE`" an
  invariant rather than a near miss.
- *No mode gate in `updateTokens()`.* The one way in is gated; the step returns
  at once on an empty array, so a Classic step allocates nothing (no `.filter`
  on an empty board). Stubbing it in the Classic pair still proves it inert.
- *`collected`*, a sixth token field, is an instrument: nothing in the
  simulation reads it. It is what lets the tests tell an expiry from a pickup
  without a spy.
- *Recharge's glyph is built on first use*, because `PURGE_GLYPH_POLY` is
  `15-render-hud.js`'s `const`, concatenated after `14-render-entities.js`, and
  is in its temporal dead zone when 14's top level runs.
- *`purgeUses` now has a second zeroing writer* (the Recharge). `02-state.js`'s
  comment and GDD §4.3 said `enterWell()` was the only one; both were corrected.
- *No per-frame allocation*: every glyph and the ring are built once; the ring's
  partial path is one array of references into `entityPoints()`' scratch, the
  HUD rings' pattern.

## P2 — Lance, Spread and the Ward

**What shipped.** The three lasting effects P1 left as unread flags (T5, T7–T9).
`06-shots.js`: `Shot` carries `pierce`, captured at fire time from
`state.powers.lance`; `fireLanes()` — the facing lane, plus both neighbours
under Spread, each through `laneNormalize()` and deduplicated; `cap`, the cap in
force (`C.SHOT_MAX` or `C.SPREAD_SHOT_MAX`), read at the gate and again per shot
in the volley. `09-collision.js`: T7's one term on the line that retires a shot,
and the Ward as one early return in `killSkimmer()` below the invulnerability
guard. `07-enemies.js`: `Thorn.chip()` split out of `onShot()`, which now reads
its argument and takes `C.LANCE_CHIP_MULT` chips, clamped by the length left.
`05-skimmer.js`: `WARD_POLY`, `wardPoints()` and `craftPoints()` — the shared
projector — and `Skimmer.draw(ctx, well, lift, ward)`. `14-render-entities.js`:
`drawShot()` takes `pierce` and strokes in `C.TOKEN_COLOR`. `00-config.js`:
`C.SFX.wardBreak` (sfx-lab candidate A, two alternates) plus
`WARD_SHELL_SCALE` 1.45 and `WARD_SHELL_ALPHA` 0.85, both ⚠. `test-cs013-p2.js`
(167 assertions). Build 689,241 → 700,942 bytes.

**Calls made while building, none of them design.**
- *Why the volley's lanes are a function and not three pushes.* `fireLanes()`
  returns a module scratch, the facing lane FIRST, so a volley a cap cut short
  would keep the lane the player aimed at. At 24 nothing is ever cut — MEASURED,
  every one of 1,500 volleys over 100 s was whole — but the order is what makes
  "partial volleys" (plan §1.6's row) a property of this build rather than an
  accident of the cap.
- *One cooldown and one `fire` sound per volley.* Spread widens the trigger
  pull; it does not speed it up, and three stacked `fire` voices on one step is
  not what GDD §14.1 describes. `tally.shotsFired` counts SHOTS, inside the
  loop, which is what the telemetry column has always meant.
- *One `chip` sound per hit, three `addScore` calls.* The hit is the event and
  the chips are the price. Splitting `Thorn.chip()` out of `onShot()` is what
  keeps `    addScore(C.PTS_THORN);` one line at four-space indent, which
  `test-cs012-p4.js:607` mutates — and what makes "5 per chip" literally true
  for a Lance chip, so both closed chip decoders read it unedited (PREDICTED at
  plan §11, MEASURED green here).
- *The clamp on a killing Lance chip.* A Thorn with one chip of length left pays
  ONE 5 and dies: the loop stops on `this.dead`. Three calls on a two-chip Thorn
  would have paid for length that was never there and turned both decoders red.
- *The shell is the craft's own outline, scaled.* `craftPoints()` is
  `skimmerPoints()`'s body with the poly and its scratch as parameters, so there
  is ONE copy of the lift math. A second projector would have been a second
  answer to "where is the craft on screen", and the shell would drift off the
  craft the first time either was retuned. `skimmerPoints()`'s signature and
  output are unchanged, to the bit.
- *The Ward's placement, read twice.* Below the guard (a declined hit does not
  spend a free hit) and above `sk.dead = true` (nothing a death does happened).
  Both directions are mutation-checked — the string that moves it above the
  guard is red, and the staged control shows an invulnerable craft keeping its
  shell.

kind: a claim written before the effect existed, restored in place.
- `test-cs013-p1.js` (P1's own, one phase old): "a death keeps the tokens and

  by the hit it absorbed, not by the death. T5's claim ("a death keeps them";
  its own text names Lance and Spread) is what the repaired assertion reads.
  ⚠ T5 and T9 interact and the plan's §11 row for P2 says "none": the

- `test-cs012-p6.js`'s Overdrive soak: "no array past `C.ENEMY_CAP` /

  read off `state.powers` — GDD §17 item 4's own rewording, applied to the soak
  that asserts it. ⛔ Not relaxed: a Classic step, and an Overdrive step without
  Spread, still read `C.SHOT_MAX` exactly.
