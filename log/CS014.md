### The three readings the plan delegated, and how they were derived

⛔ **The plan named the mechanism, the count, the depths, the payout and the
constraints, and left the ARITHMETIC of the lattice to the build.** §3's own
wording is "the arc width under RF2, and whatever RF3's lattice needs". These are
derivations inside stated constraints, not design calls — but they are written
down here because a future session will ask where the numbers came from.

**1. The crossing order and the depths.** RF3's answer is "six, evenly spaced at
`(i + 0.5) / 6`", and it lists the depths rim-first: 0.9167 … 0.0833. Laid as
`(n − i − 0.5) / n` so the array index IS the crossing order, which is the order
the take pass resolves them and the order P2 will draw them. Same six numbers.
⛔ A consequence worth its own line: every depth is strictly below 1, so the
grace beat (where `dive.depth` holds at 1) can never resolve one — the take pass
needs no phase guard, and that is arithmetic rather than luck.

**2. The arc centres.** The constraints fix almost everything. R3 forbids a
draw; R6 forbids `heat()` and the level; RF2's own argument forbids reading the
craft's lane (a set measured from where the player is standing makes its first
ring free on every dive, which is the lane-less ring again). What is left is the
WELL and constants.

`C.RING_LANE_STEP` is a **fraction of the well** rather than a lane count so the
walk asks for the same share of the rim on every well. An absolute step
degenerates on a narrow closed well: on a 5-lane Ring a step of 4 is a
`laneDelta` of −1. MEASURED off the constants at the shipped 0.25 — on the
widest well (16 lanes) the walk is 4.0 lanes per ring, and a ring's near edge is
4.0 − 1.5 = 2.5 lanes away across the 0.6083 s between crossings, or 4.1 lane/s
against `C.KEY_SPEED_MAX` 14. An attentive diver clears that; an ill-placed one
does not. ⚠ 0.5 was considered and rejected: on the widest well it demands a
half-well traverse six times in 3.65 s, which is GDD §14.5's own "turning the
breath into more work".

The walk goes through **`laneHop()`** and writes back its `dir`, as every hopper
in the roster does. `laneNormalize()` was the obvious alternative and is wrong:
it CLAMPS on an open well, and MEASURED on the 7-lane Fan at 0.25 it piles rings
4 and 5 both onto lane 6. `laneHop()` mirror-folds instead. ⚠ A fold turns the
walk around, so two rings near a wall can sit close together — that is the fold
behaving, and `test-cs014-p1.js` asserts the fold rather than the clamp by
counting how many of the six land on a wall.

**3. `C.RING_POINTS` = 100.** This is the plan's own worked figure, not a new
one: §8's sizing paragraph computes "a hypothetical 100 a ring: 600 unmultiplied
is 8.3 % of a median well". Against §1.6's MEASURED 108 wells (median 7,200,
clear bonuses mean 2,951) a full set is 8.3 % of a median well and about a fifth
of what the clear bonuses pay — near `PTS_NO_DEATH_WELL` 1,000 in weight, which
is the right size for a breath's bonus. ⚠ Provisional, owned by a tuning pass.

### The form the visual takes, and the two readings that were priced first

RF7-A's words are "the descent — **the well travelling through `dive.depth`**,
GDD §5's camera widen", and it adds "`drawWell()` already takes every parameter
it needs". Three readings were priced against those two sentences together.

- **A world ZOOM, as a canvas transform around `draw()`'s world block.** The
  obvious reading of "camera widen", and it is **MEASURED to be unavailable**.
  Keeping the widest well's rim polygon inside the fixed 1280×720 frame caps
  the zoom at **×1.10** (the Box, Ring, Cross, Clover, Vee, Heart, Star and Fan
  all land there; `WELL_CY` 330 against a 300 px rim half-height). A 10 % swell
  over 2.25 s is not a flight. Past ×1.10 the craft itself leaves the frame,
  and pillar P1's one unforgivable failure is the player not knowing where they
  are. ⛔ **This is a measurement, not a taste**, and it is recorded in GDD
  §14.5 so no later phase re-prices it.
- **A depth REMAP with nothing else.** The camera the model actually supports:
  a point at well-depth `d` drawn at `p/pc` in perspective space. It is
  correct, it is free, and ⛔ **under it the well's own outline does not move
  at all** — a tube is self-similar along its axis, so rim, throat and spokes
  are invariant and `drawWell()` genuinely needs nothing. But it moves only the
  Thorns and the rings, and **MEASURED at the plan (§1.3): 98 of 134 dives
  begin with an EMPTY board**, so three Classic dives in four would still be a
  still board. That is the exact thing RF7-A's argument exists to refuse.
- **The remap PLUS travelling rungs — shipped.** The same one function, applied
  to a lattice of `C.DIVE_RUNGS` cross-sections of the well. Each leaves at the
  rim on the step the descent reaches its own depth, so an empty Classic dive
  has ten markers rush past it and the beat reads as motion with nothing else
  on the board. ⛔ The rung lattice is `layRings()`' own formula — the midpoints
  of N equal slices — deliberately, so the descent and the rings are one family
  rather than two ideas.

### `diveDrawDepth()` comes out as `depth / diveDepth`, and is not written that way

`perspective()` is a power law, so `(d^k / dc^k)^(1/k)` is exactly `d / dc`. The
ratio is the same in either space and the direct form would be cheaper. It is
derived through `perspective()` and `invPerspective()` anyway because the CLAIM
the function makes is *"a fixed step here is a fixed step in screen distance"*
(`03-wells.js`), which is a fact about the easing and not about this
arithmetic: written as a bare ratio, a future non-power easing would silently
stop meaning it.

⛔ **The grace beat is exact by a BRANCH, not by a tolerance.** MEASURED:
`pow(pow(0.5, 0.55), 1/0.55)` comes back `0.49999999999999994`, and the claim
about that beat is bit-identity — it is also every frame outside a dive, where
`dive.depth` is 1. `if (diveDepth >= 1) return depth;` is the whole of it.

⚠ And the mapping turned out to pay for itself twice: a thing reaches the rim
**precisely** when `diveDepth` equals its own depth, which is the step
`takeRings()` resolves a ring on. What the player watches arrive is what the
take pass scored, and the drawn arc is `2 × C.RING_ARC_LANES` — the same
half-width `laneDelta` measures. Neither is a coincidence that has to be
maintained; both fall out of using the one idea.

### The driver's FOURTH clause, and why the reach test is in it

`STATUS.md`'s L11+ rule had three clauses (jump at anything aloft, never target
a `MimicShot`, go to a hovering token). CS014 adds **steer to a ring in reach
during a dive**, and ⛔ it is a no-op in Classic in the strongest sense: the
guard is `state.dive.rings.length`, which is 0 on every Classic step, so the
clause does not call into the build at all and §1's pair runs one function.

⛔ **"In reach" is the depth model, not a constant.** The descent falls 1 → 0
over `diveTime() - C.DIVE_GRACE`, so the seconds before ring *r* resolves are
`(dive.depth - r.depth) x span` and the lanes the rim axis covers in them are
that times `C.KEY_SPEED_MAX`. A ring whose NEAR edge — `C.RING_ARC_LANES` in,
the same half-width `laneDelta` measures — is outside that is **skipped**, and
the driver goes to the next one instead. ⚠ That is not politeness: chasing an
unreachable ring costs the one after it as well, which is the whole of what
"you stop earning" is meant to feel like. MEASURED: it produced **207 takes and
6 misses**, a full set cleared, and both non-vacuity halves on a played board.

### Two fixtures that had to be taught what a dive END is

⛔ **Both are the same trap in `STATUS.md` — read BEFORE the step — with one
half nobody had written down.**

1. **A run that ends INSIDE a dive leaves `dive.active` TRUE.** `Game.update()`'s
   dive branch sits below the game-over stop (`11-dive.js`), so the frozen board
   stays on screen with the dive still flagged, and the thing that finally
   clears it is the **RESTART press** — `startGame()` → `enterWell()` →
   `resetDive()`. A naive "the dive ended" detector therefore reads a menu press
   as a completed dive and its timer as the beat's length. MEASURED: 2 of 28
   "dives" in the first run, at `timer` 3.1667 against `C.DIVE_TIME_OD` 4.0.
   ⛔ **The gate is the PRE-STEP screen**: a completion is a step that BEGAN on
   `"play"`.
2. **A dive death does not always repeat the dive.** `thornDeaths` and repeats
   do not match and should not: MEASURED **11 Thorn deaths, 9 repeats and 2
   run-enders**. The identity the file asserts is `thornDeaths === repeats +
   ended`, which is GDD §5's own sentence — *a dive death that ends a run stops
   the dive too* — turned into arithmetic.

⚠ **And one accounting bug the first run found in the test, not the build:**
`state.tally` is re-minted by `startGame()`, so reading `tally.thornDeaths` at
the END of a session reads the LAST run's tally alone. It is accumulated per
step now. ⛔ The same trap is live for every counter a multi-run session reads.
