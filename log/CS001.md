## P0 — repo skeleton

Scaffolded from the Orbital Overhaul conventions, with one deliberate departure:
source is multi-file in `src/` with a concat build, rather than a single
`<script>` block. The guarantee that made the single-file rule valuable — one
artifact that is provably what ships — is preserved by making `dist/` the
behaviour oracle the tests load.

`build.js` checks its manifest in both directions. See `DECISIONS.md`.

## P3 — static well rendering + well-lab · closing phase

`src/13-render-well.js` draws the well through exactly two primitives —
`drawPoly` (path only, no fill) and `glowStroke` (two `lighter`-composited
strokes, wide-and-dim then thin-and-bright) — matching GDD 10.2's "no
per-entity pipelines, no fills, no sprites" rule from day one. `drawWell`
projects a well's rim and throat polygons through `C.WELL_CX/CY/RADIUS` (the
same mapping `screenPos` uses for entities), strokes the two rings, then walks
every vertex index once to draw the shared rim→throat spoke — a spoke bounds
up to two lanes, so lighting for shared spokes takes either neighbour, and
open-well end spokes correctly see only one neighbour.

Band colour (GDD 3.6) and the dim band (GDD 3.7) landed as pure functions,
`wellBandColor(level, rng)` and `wellBaseAlpha(level)`, both driven by new `C`
knobs (`BAND_COLORS`, `BAND_RNG_COLORS`, `DIM_BAND_LO/HI`) rather than inline
literals. Depth-varying line weight (`laneLineWidth`) is linear between
`LINE_W_THROAT` and `LINE_W_RIM` — GDD 10.1's explicit divergence from vector
hardware. Lane lighting on occupancy/shot-travel/Surger-charge is wired
through `drawWell`'s `laneState` parameter and does nothing yet, since no
caller has a lit lane to pass — that wiring is for shots/enemies to pick up
without a renderer change.

Since `02-state.js` and `23-main.js` are still CS002 territory, the "debug key
cycles all sixteen wells" requirement is met by a small self-contained
bootstrap at the bottom of `13-render-well.js`: it owns nothing but a local
well index, listens for "W", and steps out of the way (it's dead code, not a
competing loop) the moment CS002's real loop exists and starts calling
`drawWell` itself. Guarded on `typeof window/document` so the headless harness
never runs it.

`tools/well-lab.html` duplicates the well data (embedded as JSON) and the
render slice needed to preview `PERSPECTIVE_EXP`, `THROAT_SCALE`, and every
glow constant live against a canvas, plus a dim-band preview toggle and a
lit-lane-count slider — all sliders are readouts for hand-porting into
`src/00-config.js`, never a write path into the build.

`scratchpad/test-cs001-p3.js` asserts the band palette against GDD 3.6's
table (including the past-99 RNG palette), the dim band's exact window and
alpha, line-weight monotonicity, and that `drawWell` never throws across all
sixteen wells at three representative levels/lane-lighting states. Build and
suite green: 24 modules, 5 test files.

CS001 closes here. GDD 3, 3.6, 3.7, and 10.2 are now load-bearing on shipped
code; CS002 (loop, input, Skimmer, shots) starts from a well that can already
draw itself.
