## P0 — repo skeleton

Scaffolded from the Orbital Overhaul conventions, with one deliberate departure:
source is multi-file in `src/` with a concat build, rather than a single
`<script>` block. The guarantee that made the single-file rule valuable — one
artifact that is provably what ships — is preserved by making `dist/` the
behaviour oracle the tests load.

`build.js` checks its manifest in both directions. See `DECISIONS.md`.
