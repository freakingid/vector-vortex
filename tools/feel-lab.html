feel-lab

Measures traverse-and-stop quality — P1's requirement is a player must be able to whip a third of the way around the well and stop exactly on a lane, on every device. This does not render anything; it runs a synthetic control policy through each device's duplicated response curve and reports numbers to read off and port into src/00-config.js.

Settle epsilon is 0.05 lane units. "No settle" means the policy never held inside epsilon for the rest of the run within the tick cap — read that as a real failure at that value, not a slow success. Keyboard/D-pad overshoot is expected to sit near zero: releasing a held key stops movement on the very next tick (no coast), so a competent stop policy has nothing to overshoot with — that is the tap/hold design working, not a gap in this instrument.
