The solo test (GDD 11.4(c)). SOLO one layer. If it is not recognisable played alone — a tune, a line, a figure, a groove — mark it FAIL. Only a PASS layer may be tiered. SOLO is additive: solo two layers to hear them as a pair. Marks, tiers, gains, cutoffs and tempo travel back only through COPY TABLE.
Intensity and tier. INTENSITY is the director's level, 0 to 1. A tiered layer's gate opens at its threshold () and closes below it, on the next bar line. The sweep (a low-pass, ) follows at once. The limiter is always on, as in the game.
